## Findings

### [must-fix]

**MF-1. Slice-table Deps column vs DAG-shape line: two different edge sets (S4, S6).**
- S6 cell (Slice series table, L145): Deps = "**S1–S5**" (en-dash range → S1,S2,S3,S4,S5). DAG-shape line (L147): "`S0 → {S1, S2, S3}`; `S2 → S4 → S5 → S6`; `S1, S3 → S6`" — explicit S6 deps are {S1, S3, S5}; S2/S4 reach S6 only transitively.
- S4 cell (L143): Deps = "**S0, S2**". DAG line gives S4 only {S2} (S0 transitive via S2).
- Why must-fix: review-round-1's disposition claimed "the DAG is single-sourced (slice-table deps = DAG-shape line)" — no longer literally true. A create-beads pass reading the table builds S6 with direct S2/S4 edges; one reading the DAG line does not: two different bead DAGs from one doc. Mitigating: transitive closures are identical (no scheduling or parallel-branch divergence) and a safe default exists (the DAG line's reduced form) — but the final round should not hand create-beads two answers.
- Suggested fix (two cell edits): S4 Deps → `S2`; S6 Deps → `S5, S1, S3`. Alternative: one clause after the DAG line — "Deps column lists transitively-reduced direct edges; create-beads models the closure." Slices: S4, S6.

**MF-2. The `>400` floor replacement (per-file registration accounting + loose liveness floor) is promised three times and owned by no slice.**
- KC 12 (L313–316): "magic `>400` floor replaced by per-file registration accounting + loose liveness floor" — the "(per-slice)" header describes the shim-map ratchet cadence, not an owner for this one-time policy change.
- Data Model 6 (L509–511): "registration accounting replaces the `>400` floor."
- Risks row "Spec silent-drop" (L544): "Registration accounting + loose floor (data leg D5-c); per-slice ratchet."
- TESTS item 4 lists "count-floor policy changes" as a Harness Δ item **type**, but no slice cell (S0–S6) carries one; S2/S4/S5 cells name only spec-conversion + jquery-site carriers.
- Why must-fix: lens-3's "outcome clause with no owner slice." Create-beads must invent the owner (guess: S2 first harness Δ, or S0); different guesses produce different convoys, and the un-guessed case silently drops a promised spec-silent-drop mitigation.
- Suggested fix: pin it. S2 cell Harness Δ clause += "count-floor policy: magic `>400` replaced by per-file registration accounting + loose liveness floor in this slice's harness commit"; mirror in TESTS item 4 ("S2 lands the policy change; S4/S5 extend the accounting per conversion"). (S0 alternative if the accounting must predate any conversion — but S0 is the pinched slice at 10–11.) Slices: S2 owner, S4/S5 extend.
