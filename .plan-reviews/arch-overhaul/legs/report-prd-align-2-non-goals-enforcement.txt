### [guardrail] F5 — page__conditional-comment deletion needs an authorization record

- Section: Slice table S6 "`page__conditional-comment` deleted (IE relic)";
  KC13 same.
- Traces to: NG-1 (removal shrinks the templating story's surface).
- Evidence: desktop.blocks/page/__conditional-comment/ carries bemhtml + bh
  (+ ru.md) ✓; IE conditional comments execute only in IE≤9 — dead under C5
  (evergreen-only).
- Why guardrail, not cut: CONST-P6 forces move-or-delete for every platform
  standalone; hoisting an IE-only relic into common.blocks would enshrine
  dead code; on C5-supported engines the deletion is behavior-neutral.
- Proposed edit: S6 row/KC13 gains "(authorized template removal: C5
  evergreen-only ⇒ IE-only elem is dead code; MIGRATION entry names the elem
  and both template files; hoist-to-common rejected as dead-code-in-common)".

- Evidence: `ua__dom` reads `ios`, `android`, `bada`, `wp`, `opera`,
